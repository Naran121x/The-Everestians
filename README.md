## 🚀 About NASA Space Apps Challenge

The **NASA International Space Apps Challenge** is an annual global hackathon organized by NASA in collaboration with space agencies and partners around the world.

It brings together students, developers, scientists, designers, and creative thinkers to solve real-world challenges using open data, technology, and innovative ideas.

For the **2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge**, our team took on the challenge:

> **"Abandoned but not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars."**

The challenge focuses on the stories of equipment left behind during lunar and Martian exploration, encouraging participants to explore their history, purpose, and scientific significance.

We approached this challenge through a combination of technology, research, and interactive storytelling.

## 🌑 About the Project

*Humanity's exploration of the Moon and Mars has left behind more than footprints and flags.*

Throughout decades of exploration, spacecraft, landers, rovers, scientific instruments, and other equipment have reached these distant worlds. Some completed their missions successfully, while others became inactive or remained behind after their objectives were fulfilled.

But their stories did not end there.

Each artifact represents an era of scientific ambition, engineering, experimentation, and discovery. They are reminders of humanity's determination to explore worlds beyond our own.

**Abandoned but Not Forgotten** brings these artifacts back into the spotlight through an interactive digital museum.

Instead of presenting space equipment as isolated technical objects, we give visitors an opportunity to explore their histories, understand their purposes, and discover the missions behind them.

Our mission is simple:

## 🎯 The Problem We're Addressing

Space exploration is often remembered through successful launches, historic landings, groundbreaking discoveries, and iconic photographs.

However, the equipment that made these achievements possible receives far less attention.

Many people are unaware of:

1. What equipment remains on the Moon and Mars.
2. Why these objects were sent there.
3. What their original missions were.
4. Where these artifacts are located.
5. What scientific discoveries they contributed to.
6. Why they continue to matter today.

We wanted to make this overlooked part of space exploration history more accessible, engaging, and interesting to discover.

## 💡 Our Solution

We developed an **interactive digital space museum** where visitors can explore the history of artifacts associated with NASA's lunar and Martian exploration.

The platform combines 3D models, planetary exploration, visual storytelling, mission histories, and educational information.

Rather than simply listing facts, the museum gives each artifact context — its purpose, its journey, its mission, and its contribution to our understanding of space.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🪐 01. Planetary Atlas
Explore the Moon and Mars through a dedicated planetary experience. Discover the worlds and explore the locations associated with historic missions and artifacts.

### 🏛️ 02. Interactive Exhibit Gallery
Browse a collection of space exploration artifacts and uncover their individual histories, purposes, and scientific significance.

### 🧊 03. Interactive 3D Models
Get closer to space exploration through interactive 3D models. Examine selected spacecraft and equipment using GLB-based 3D assets.

### 🌕 04. Moon Exploration
Discover the history of lunar exploration and learn about the equipment and missions that contributed to humanity's exploration of the Moon.

### 🔴 05. Mars Exploration
Travel through the history of robotic exploration on Mars, including landers, rovers, helicopters, and scientific instruments.

### ⏳ 06. Mission Timeline
Explore important milestones in space exploration through a timeline connecting missions, launches, and discoveries.

### ⚖️ 07. Compare View
Compare selected artifacts to understand their differences, purposes, and roles in planetary exploration.

### 📱 08. Responsive Experience
Explore the museum through an interface designed to provide an engaging experience across desktop and mobile devices.

Space exploration is not just about reaching new worlds. It is about the discoveries, experiments, engineering, and knowledge that remain long after a mission ends.
